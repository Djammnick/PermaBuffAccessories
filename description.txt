Permabuff Accessories is a mod that adds several items that enable permanent version of many potion buffs.

List of Contents:

- Accessories applying permanent buffs in 4 tiers to ultimately combine many into one.
- A new enemy which guards a key ingredient for binding the effects.
- Two new dedicated crafting stations for crafting the aforementioned accessories.

The accessories come in 4 tiers:
-> Tier I is available after the Eye of Cthulhu, using a drop from an elusive enemy and the Demonite or Crimtane Brewery.
-> Tier II is available as soon as you obtain Alchemy Station.
-> Tier III is available as soon as you obtain the Living Brewery, which drops from a powerful plant menace.
-> Tier IV - the final accessory - is available at the end of your journey.
